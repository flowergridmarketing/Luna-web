## H2: Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a nutritionist and a Registered Nutritional Therapist?  
A Registered Nutritional Therapist has undergone extensive clinical training and is often registered with professional bodies like BANT or the CNHC. They focus on the functional and metabolic roots of health issues, providing a deeper, more clinical level of Nutritional Consulting than a general nutritionist.

Does Nutritional Consulting involve taking a lot of expensive supplements?  
No. At Flowergrid, we follow a "food-first" philosophy. While we may suggest specific supplements to address a clinical deficiency or support a particular health goal, the focus is always on making sustainable changes to your daily diet.

Can Nutritional Consulting help with specific conditions like IBS or Type 2 Diabetes?  
Yes. Nutritional Consulting is highly effective for managing metabolic and digestive issues. We work to identify the root causes of your symptoms and create a plan that supports your body's natural healing processes and long-term health.

How long does it take to see results?  
Many clients notice an improvement in their energy and digestion within the first two to four weeks. However, deeper metabolic or hormonal shifts typically take three to six months of consistent habit change and support.

Do I have to give up my traditional or cultural foods?  
Absolutely not. We pride ourselves on offering culturally sensitive Nutritional Consulting. We work with you to make your traditional foods work for your health, ensuring your plan is realistic and enjoyable for your lifestyle.

Do you offer functional testing, such as blood or gut health tests?  
Yes. Where appropriate, your practitioner may recommend private functional testing to gain deeper insight into your microbiome or metabolic health. We will explain the benefits and costs of these tests clearly before you proceed.
