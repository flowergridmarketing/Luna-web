## Frequently Asked Questions

How does Craniosacral Therapy work with the central nervous system?  
Craniosacral Therapy is a gentle manual therapy that focuses on the subtle rhythmic flow of cerebrospinal fluid. By using a very light touch, a practitioner helps release restrictions in the fascia and the membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord. This process supports the central nervous system to self-correct, allowing your body to settle into a state of deep restoration and balance.

What is the difference between Craniosacral Therapy and traditional massage?  
While both are body-based treatments, the methodology is very different. Traditional massage involves the manipulation of surface muscles through pressure. Craniosacral Therapy is a non-invasive, subtle practice that works with the deeper tissues and the nervous system. It does not use force or deep pressure, making it an ideal choice for those who find traditional massage too intense or stimulating.

Can Craniosacral Therapy help with anxiety and the vagus nerve?  
Yes. This therapy is highly effective for nervous system regulation. By encouraging the body to move out of a "fight or flight" response, it supports the function of the vagus nerve, which is responsible for your body's ability to rest and digest. Many clients notice a significant reduction in the physical symptoms of anxiety, such as a racing heart or shallow breathing, after a session.

Can this therapy provide relief for TMJ and jaw tension?  
Absolutely. Craniosacral Therapy is often sought out by individuals struggling with TMJ (temporomandibular joint) issues, teeth grinding, or chronic jaw clenching. By gently addressing the tension in the bones and fascia of the skull and face, the therapy helps the jaw muscles relax, which can also lead to a reduction in stress-related headaches and migraines.

Are there any conditions where Craniosacral Therapy is not recommended?  
While it is exceptionally gentle and safe for most people, there are a few specific contraindications. It is not suitable for individuals with conditions where a slight change in intracranial pressure could be dangerous, such as a recent skull fracture, a brain haemorrhage, or an acute stroke. We always recommend discussing your medical history during your discovery call to ensure it is the right approach for you.

Do I remain fully clothed during the treatment?  
Yes. You remain fully clothed throughout your entire Craniosacral Therapy session. We suggest wearing comfortable, loose-fitting clothing to help you feel completely at ease while lying on the treatment table. Because there is no oil or deep tissue manipulation involved, you can easily transition back into your day immediately after your appointment.
