## Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP)?  
NLP is a practical way of understanding how your thoughts, language, and patterns of behaviour affect your life. By becoming aware of these subconscious "programmes," you can learn to rewrite them, helping you break old habits and build a more confident, resilient mindset.

How is NLP different from traditional talk therapy?  
While therapy often looks back at the past to understand why you feel a certain way, NLP is generally more future-focused. It provides you with specific tools and techniques to change how you think and act in the present, leading to faster, action-oriented shifts.

How many sessions will I need before I see a change?  
Everyone is different, but many clients notice a shift in their perspective after just two or three sessions. For lasting transformation and the building of new, sustainable habits, we typically recommend a structured programme of six to twelve sessions.

NLP is highly effective for overcoming self-doubt, managing anxiety, and breaking cycles of procrastination. It is also widely used for improving communication skills, building professional confidence, and resolving the internal conflicts that keep you feeling stuck or indecisive.
