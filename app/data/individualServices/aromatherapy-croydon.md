## Frequently Asked Questions

What makes clinical aromatherapy different from using oils at home?  
Clinical aromatherapy at Flowergrid involves a professional consultation and bespoke blending of therapeutic-grade oils. We target your specific health needs and biological profiles to ensure a precise physiological response.

How does aromatherapy help with stress and anxiety?  
When you inhale essential oils, the molecules speak directly to the limbic system, the brain's emotional centre. This rapidly signals your nervous system to move from a stress response into a state of calm.

Can aromatherapy support me during menopause or hormonal changes?  
Yes. Specific botanical oils like clary sage and geranium are highly effective for balancing mood and managing symptoms such as hot flushes, helping you navigate transitions with greater ease.

Are there any safety concerns with using essential oils?  
When guided by a professional, aromatherapy is very safe. However, certain oils must be avoided with specific medical conditions or medications. We review your history to ensure every blend is safe for you.

Can I have aromatherapy sessions while I am pregnant?  
Yes, but with care. Some oils must be avoided entirely during pregnancy. Our clinical aromatherapists in Croydon are trained to provide safe, supportive care that is appropriate for both you and your baby.

Do I need to visit the Croydon centre for aromatherapy?  
For the most effective sensory experience, we recommend in-person sessions at our sanctuary in Coulsdon Rd. However, we can provide online guidance on how to use botanical oils as part of your home routine.
