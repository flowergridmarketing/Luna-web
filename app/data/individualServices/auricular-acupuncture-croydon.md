## H2: Frequently Asked Questions

Does Auricular Acupuncture hurt?  
Most people find the treatment very comfortable. The needles used are extremely fine, much thinner than a standard medical needle. You might feel a tiny pinch or a sensation of warmth, but many clients actually fall asleep during the treatment.

What are ear seeds and how do they work?  
Ear seeds are small, non-invasive beads that are taped to specific points on the ear. They allow you to stimulate the points yourself by applying gentle pressure, extending the benefits of the treatment after you leave the centre.

Can Auricular Acupuncture help with weight loss?  
It can be a very effective supporting tool. By targeting points related to digestion, metabolism, and cravings, it helps you manage the emotional and physical aspects of changing your eating habits.

How many sessions of ear acupuncture will I need?  
For acute stress or a quick "reset," one session can be very beneficial. For chronic pain or habit change, we typically recommend a course of five to ten sessions to achieve and maintain the best results.

Is it safe to have Auricular Acupuncture if I am pregnant?  
While it is generally very safe, there are certain points that should be avoided during pregnancy. It is essential to inform your practitioner if you are pregnant so they can tailor the treatment safely for you.

Do I need to do anything to prepare for my session?  
We recommend having a light snack before your appointment and avoiding caffeine immediately before the session. This helps your nervous system stay relaxed and receptive to the treatment.
