### **Example code:**

export default async function sitemap() {  
 const baseUrl \= "https://flowergrid.co.uk";

 // Example: static pages

   "",  
   "/shop",  
   "/about",  
   "/contact",  
 \].map((route) \=\> ({

 // Example: blog posts (replace with your real data source)  
 const blogPosts \= \[  
   { slug: "first-post" },

   url: \`${baseUrl}/blog/${post.slug}\`,  
   lastModified: new Date(),  
 }));

 return \[...staticPages, ...blogPosts\];  
}

## Frequently Asked Questions

What is a vitamin and mineral body scan and how does it work?  
The body scan we use is a non-invasive tool that measures the bio-impedance or resonance of your body’s tissues. It provides a real-time assessment of your current vitamin and mineral levels. By identifying these biological markers, we can pinpoint exactly which nutrients your body is lacking and create a plan to restore your natural balance.

Does a session with a Healthcare Consultant in Croydon replace my regular GP?  
No. My role at Flowergrid is as a specialist consultant focused on preventative health, nutrition, and wellness. While I provide detailed health audits and vitamin checks, I always recommend that you maintain a relationship with your GP for any acute medical conditions or formal diagnoses. Our work is designed to complement traditional medicine.

Is the body scan procedure painful or invasive?  
Not at all. The body scan is completely painless and requires no needles or blood samples. You simply hold a sensor or place your hands on the scanning pads for a few minutes. It is a quick and comfortable process that provides immediate data about your internal health without any discomfort.

How does having my biological data help with the life coaching process?  
Many people struggle with "mindset" blocks like low motivation, anxiety, or brain fog that are actually rooted in physical depletion. By looking at your vitamin and mineral data, we can ensure your body is chemically supported. This creates a stronger physical foundation, making the life coaching and habit changes we work on much more effective and easier to maintain.

Can I have a body scan during an online consultation?  
While our life coaching and initial health discussions are highly effective via Zoom, the physical body scan must be performed in person. You will need to visit our Croydon centre in Coulsdon to have the scan. Many clients book an in-person session for the data collection and then continue their ongoing coaching journey online.

What specific health concerns can a health audit and body scan address?  
A consultation is particularly helpful if you are experiencing persistent fatigue, digestive issues, "brain fog," or a general sense of being physically out of balance. It is also an excellent tool for those interested in longevity and preventative health who want to ensure their nutrition is perfectly tailored to their body's requirements.
